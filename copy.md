# Career View Launch Copy

## Onboarding Headline
Choose your CS career path with confidence, not guesswork.

## Onboarding Subheadline
Career View compares realistic 3-10 year outcomes across supported tech roles and specializations.

## What Career View Does
Career View helps you evaluate Computer Science careers using structured inputs and market-grounded simulation logic. It shows your fit score, salary trajectory, demand outlook, and stability so you can make a clearer career decision.

## What Career View Does NOT Do
Career View does not guarantee job offers, exact salaries, or perfect outcomes. It does not currently cover all industries or every career role outside the supported CS scope.

## Standard Human Disclaimer
Career View simulates realistic outcomes based on current market data. Results are guidance, not guarantees.

---

## Error and Edge Case Copy

### Unsupported Career
This role is not supported yet. Career View currently focuses on CS careers.

### Unsupported Specialization
This specialization is not supported yet for the selected career family.

### Insufficient Skills Selected
Select at least 2 skills to generate a reliable result.

### Empty Comparison
Choose at least one supported career to run a comparison.

### Low Confidence Output
Confidence is low for this result. Treat this as directional guidance and validate with projects, mentors, and real job trends.

## Tooltip and Microcopy

### Career Fit Score Tooltip
A personalized score based on skill alignment, salary progression, demand outlook, and stability.

### Confidence Level Tooltip
Shows how strong the signal is for your selected role. Core CS roles are more reliable than edge cases.

### Why no free-text input?
We use guided selections to keep your result consistent, comparable, and trustworthy.

---

## Why Scope Is Intentionally Limited
Career View v1 focuses on CS careers and CS specializations because accuracy and trust are more important than broad coverage. A smaller, honest product gives better guidance than a larger product with weak signals.
